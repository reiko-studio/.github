# Reiko Studio

**Solving problems, occasionally creating new ones.**

Reiko Studio is a personal software workshop for scientific computing,
development, and the tools that make both a little easier.

Most projects start with a simple thought: *surely there's a better way to do
this.* Sometimes the answer is a small utility. Sometimes it is a much larger
project than originally planned. And more often than not, it was already solved
somehow, somewhere. These things happen.

<img src="../assets/reiko-sakura.png" alt="Reiko sitting thoughtfully in a pink hoodie" width="360">

## What we (I) do

The studio is home to a growing collection of tools and experiments:

- **Scientific computing**: simulations, numerical analysis, and visualization.
- **Development**: tools for everyday programming and project workflows.
- **Systems and infrastructure**: Linux utilities, automation, and computational resources.
- **Experiments**: ideas worth trying, even when they get a little out of hand.

The aim is straightforward: build software that is useful, understandable, and
pleasant to work with.

## Meet Reiko

Reiko Hiramoto (平本 玲子) is the face of Reiko Studio. She's 27, quiet,
curious, and observant, with a habit of noticing the little things.

She can be hard to read at first and tends to keep her distance around
strangers. With people she trusts, she's affectionate, playful, and fond of a
little teasing. She enjoys peaceful moments, comfortable clothes, and seeing
where her curiosity takes her.
